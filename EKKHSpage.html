Elisha Kent Kane Historical Society

The Elisha Kent Kane Historical Society is housed in the Masonic Hall on W 23rd St New York, NY. The historical society is dedicated to polar exploration and the explorations of Elish Kent Kane. The collection contains books, artifacts, and paintings.

Riley began volunteering for the historical society in July 2026 as an archivist to help organize and maintian the collection.
