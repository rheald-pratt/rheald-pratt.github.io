The Ohio State University Archvies

Riley volunteered for two years in the Ohio State University Archives alongside assistant archivist Kevlin Haire.

The first project was to process the collection from the Office of Diversity and Inclusion. This project included various phases:

  1. Appraisal and acquisition of materials
  2. Research and sorting materials
  3. Rehousing collection materials
  4. Entering collection metadata into Archivist Toolkit
  5. Shelving Collection

Other Responsibilities:

  • Blog posts
  • Outreach events
  • Weeding the publicaations collection of duplicate materials

Skills gained

  • Archivist Toolkit
  • organizational skills
  • outreach
  • appraisal and accession
  • reseach and organizational skills
