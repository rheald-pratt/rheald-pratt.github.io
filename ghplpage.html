Grandview Heights Public Library

While living in Columbus, Ohio, Riley worked at the Grandview Heights Public Library as a Library Associate. GHPL is a school district public library with around 17,000 registered patrons.

The Grandview Heights Public Library offers services including notary services, printing/scanning, meeting rooms, children and adult programming, computer access, free wifi, laptop borrowing. Lends materials including books, DVDs, CDS, LPs, board games, craft materials, film converter, metal detector, etc.
